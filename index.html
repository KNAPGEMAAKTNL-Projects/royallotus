Good To Know
Questions, answered
How soon can you arrive?

Same-day bookings are usually possible. Within Tangier we typically arrive within 60 to 90 minutes; for Cap Spartel, Ksar es-Seghir or Asilah, book a few hours ahead.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing at all. We bring the massage table, fresh linens, oils and calming music. A quiet corner of about two by three metres is all we need.

Do you come to hotels and riads?

Yes. Hotels, riads, holiday villas, apartments and private homes across Tangier and the surrounding coast. We are discreet and used to working with hotel reception desks.

Can we book as a couple?

Absolutely, it is our signature. Both therapists arrive together so you are treated side by side at the same time. You can also book them both for yourself as a four-hand massage.

How do you handle hygiene?

Linens and towels are replaced after every session, all equipment is disinfected, and our therapists follow a strict professional code throughout.

How can I pay?

Cash in dirham or euro after your session, or bank transfer. No prepayment is needed; every booking is confirmed personally on WhatsApp.

Which languages do you speak?

You can arrange everything in English, French or Dutch.
